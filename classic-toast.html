Classic Korean Street Toast

Makes 2 sandwiches10 minutes1 large pan

This is the toast you smell before you see it, sold from carts near subway exits on cold mornings: buttery bread, a thick omelet packed with shredded cabbage, a sprinkle of sugar, and a stripe of ketchup. It sounds like a strange mix until you try it. Sweet, salty, crunchy and soft all at once, and it takes about ten minutes.

Ingredients

  • 4 slices soft white bread
  • 2 tbsp butter, plus more for the pan
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 packed cup finely shredded green cabbage
  • ¼ carrot, shredded
  • 1 scallion, thinly sliced
  • Pinch of salt
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • Ketchup

Steps

  1. Whisk the eggs in a bowl, then stir in the cabbage, carrot, scallion and salt. It should look like more vegetables than egg.
  2. Heat a large pan over medium. Swipe butter across the surface and toast the bread on both sides until golden. Move the bread to a plate.
  3. Add a little more butter. Spoon the egg mixture into two rough squares about the size of a slice of bread, and press them flat with a spatula.
  4. Cook 2 to 3 minutes per side, until both sides are browned and the middle is set.
  5. Lay each omelet on a slice of toast. Sprinkle a teaspoon of sugar over each, add a zigzag of ketchup, and close with the second slice.
  6. Wrap the bottom half in a paper towel or wax paper and eat it hot.

Tips

  • Hold the butter by its wrapper and swipe it straight across the hot pan. It's faster than slicing pats and coats the pan evenly.
  • Shred the cabbage as thin as you can. Thin shreds bind into the egg; thick ones fall out.
  • Don't skip the sugar. It's what makes this taste like the cart version.

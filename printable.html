Sugar-Crust Street Toast

You need

  • 4 slices white bread, 3 tbsp soft butter, 3 tbsp sugar
  • 2 eggs, 1 cup shredded cabbage, ¼ carrot, salt
  • Ketchup

Do this

  1. Mix eggs, cabbage, carrot, salt.
  2. Butter bread, press buttered side into sugar.
  3. Toast sugar-side down, medium-low, 2–3 min.
  4. Cook egg mix as two squares, 2–3 min per side.
  5. Sandwich with sugared sides out, ketchup inside.

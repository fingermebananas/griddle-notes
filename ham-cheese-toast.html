Ham & Cheese Street Toast

Makes 2 sandwiches12 minutes1 large pan

The loaded version of the cart classic. Same cabbage omelet and same sugar-and-ketchup finish, plus a slice of ham warmed on the griddle, a square of melty cheese, and a thin layer of mayo. It's the one most stalls charge an extra dollar for, and it's worth it.

Ingredients

  • 4 slices soft white bread
  • 2 tbsp butter, plus more for the pan
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 packed cup finely shredded green cabbage
  • 1 scallion, thinly sliced
  • Pinch of salt
  • 2 slices deli ham
  • 2 slices American or cheddar cheese
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • Mayonnaise and ketchup

Steps

  1. Whisk the eggs with the cabbage, scallion and salt.
  2. Butter the hot pan and toast the bread on both sides. Set it aside.
  3. Add more butter and cook the egg mixture as two bread-sized squares, 2 to 3 minutes per side.
  4. While the omelets cook, lay the ham on an empty corner of the pan for about 30 seconds per side.
  5. Build each sandwich: toast, thin layer of mayo, omelet, ham, cheese, a teaspoon of sugar, ketchup, toast.
  6. Press the sandwich back onto the pan for 20 seconds so the cheese starts to melt, then wrap and eat.

Tips

  • Put the cheese directly on the hot ham so it melts on contact.
  • Go light on the mayo. It's there for richness, not as a sauce.
